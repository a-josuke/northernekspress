About Us

A Papua New Guinean company, built to last

Northern Ekspress Limited is a fully Papua New Guinean-owned civil works, heavy equipment and plant hire company delivering practical infrastructure solutions across Papua New Guinea.

Northern Ekspress Limited is a fully Papua New Guinean-owned civil works, heavy equipment and plant hire company based in East Sepik Province. We deliver practical, reliable and cost-effective infrastructure solutions for government, private sector and community projects.

  • Fully Papua New Guinean Owned
  • Practical, Reliable Solutions

We provide reliable, cost-effective civil construction, house building, heavy equipment and plant hire services across Papua New Guinea, delivering practical solutions that meet the needs of government, private sector and community projects, on time and within budget.

  • Skilled Operators & Equipment
  • Commitment to Safety & Quality

To become one of Papua New Guinea's most trusted and capable indigenous civil works, residential construction and plant hire companies, delivering infrastructure and homes that genuinely connect communities and support long-term economic growth.

  • Trusted Local Partner
  • Long-Term Community Growth

Our History

Company Journey

Our Beginnings

Northern Ekspress Limited began as a small, fully Papua New Guinean-owned outfit taking on local civil works and transport jobs in East Sepik Province, built on hard work and strong community relationships.

  • Fully Papua New Guinean Owned
  • Based in East Sepik Province
Building Our Fleet

We invested in heavy tipper trucks, a backhoe loader, Toyota LandCruiser/4WD support vehicles and compactors, allowing us to take on larger road construction and civil works contracts.

  • Reliable Equipment & Fleet
  • Skilled Operators
Expanding Our Services

We grew beyond civil works into building construction, agroforestry and timber milling, and agriculture commodities export, broadening what we could offer government, private and community clients.

  • Building Construction
  • Agroforestry & Agri-Export
Growing Our Team

We brought together a leadership team and skilled field crew committed to safety, quality and reliable delivery, from project planning through to machine operation and mechanical support.

  • Experienced Leadership Team
  • Skilled Field Crew
Looking Ahead

Today we continue expanding our reach, pursuing partnerships with government agencies, District Development Authorities and industry to deliver larger provincial and national infrastructure projects.

  • Growing Provincial Reach
  • Open to Partnerships & JVs
